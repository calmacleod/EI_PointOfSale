What changed, and why

Decision
Was
Now
Navigation
224px labelled sidebar
44px icon rail + ⌘K palette — 180px back to the data, and faster for staff who live in five screens
Page identity
18px title + description block, ~60px tall, on every page
One 34px command bar carrying breadcrumb, live drawer state, search, and the primary action
Containers
Rounded cards floating on grey with 8px gaps and shadows
Edge-to-edge panels divided by 1px rules. No radius, no shadow, no gutter
Chrome
Light sidebar, light page — everything one tone
Dark chrome, white data. You always know whether you are looking at the machine or the record
Table rows
49px, wrapping, ~5 rows visible
26px, nowrap, ~24 rows visible — the density you actually asked for
Record state
A pill in its own column
3px stripe on the row edge plus a 10px uppercase label — a column reclaimed, and scannable down the page
Machine data
Same font as prose
Monospace with tabular figures for codes, quantities, money — misreading a barcode gets much harder
Keyboard
Undocumented
Key hints everywhere: on buttons, on tender keys, and in a persistent status bar. F-keys are how a till is actually driven
Colour
Teal, plus stray Tailwind blues, plus five pill colours
Teal for action only. Every other colour reports a fact about a record
