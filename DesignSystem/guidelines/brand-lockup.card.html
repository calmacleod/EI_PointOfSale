The mark

The E is three bars on a spine — a table's row rules. The I is the 3px state stripe. 32-unit grid, 4-unit weight, 5-unit gaps.

Sizes in use

16px minimum · 26px rail · 32px sign-in. Clear space equals the stripe width at that size (mark ÷ 8).

Backgrounds

Two-colour on white and chrome. One-colour on the accent and in print — the stripe takes the ink.
